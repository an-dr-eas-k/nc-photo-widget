Shows a random picture from a folder on your Nextcloud server as a home screen widget, without syncing your photo library to the phone. Only one downscaled preview is stored at a time.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Any number of widgets, each with its own folders, tags, interval, shape and filter</li>
<li>Random photo from one or more Nextcloud folders, subfolders included</li>
<li>Tag filter: photos with any of some tags and all of others</li>
<li>Time span filter: photos of the last days, optionally also of the same days in every earlier year</li>
<li>Widget shapes: rounded corners, square, circle, oval, pill or cut corners; resizable down to one tile</li>
<li>Picture changes automatically every 15 minutes to 24 hours, or only on tap</li>
<li>Configurable photo list refresh, with photo count, last sync time and sync duration</li>
<li>Picture filters such as grayscale, sepia or a faded film look; pictures with "snapseed" in the file name are shown unfiltered, as they already have a look</li>
<li>Tap the left edge for the previous picture, the right edge for the next one; the recent history is kept on the device, also for offline use</li>
<li>Tap the center to open the picture in the official Nextcloud app</li>
<li>Light, dark or system theme</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
The app only talks to the Nextcloud server you configure. Login uses Nextcloud Login Flow v2, so a revocable app password is stored on the device and never your real password. There is no tracking and no analytics.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer and a Nextcloud server reachable over HTTPS. This is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with Nextcloud GmbH.
